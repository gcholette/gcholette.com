I recently got inspired by the text-based BBS ezines of the 80s/90s. It had 
been a while since i needed to update my homepage / blog and this is the 
aesthetic I chose to go with. I wanted something minimal and low-tech (no 
framework or server). Blog posts / ctf writeups would be written as pure ascii 
text files and rendered inside a 80 character wide container, taking inspiration 
from retro terminal monitors.

Regarding the tech, I really wanted a serverless dependency-free setup, even 
tough it's more hands-on. So I'm going with just raw html/css/js. I'll have to 
copy paste major changes all over as the downside. The setup probably won't 
scale but it probably doesn't need to anyways. 

All of the code, contents, formatting and ASCII "art" are to be written without 
*any* use of AI. It's important for me in the current context to write with 
intentionality and to keep it raw. For every article I read online, I'm always 
scrutinizing to see if it was human written or not. I know i'm a bit obsessed 
with it, but I think i'm not the only one wishing there was less AI in this 
thing i'm really passionate about, and that the craft is kept alive.

Articles will be posted sporadically here, some years will go by without any new
article and others will benefit from a motivation boost. All the stuff on this 
site is just for me to serve as either a outlet, publish technical knowledge, 
CTF writeups or projects. The articles before this date were written in markdown
and ported by hand loosely to the current format.
